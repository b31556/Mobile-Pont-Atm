For card holders

Pont Card

Your points, everything that happened to them, and the event program, right on your phone. No account, nothing to sign up for.

  • Hold your card to the back of the phone to add it. The app keeps one card: yours
  • Your photo and your points, refreshed whenever you open it
  • Every point you got, check-in and check-out, day by day
  • The program: like events, drag them onto your day, choose how long you stay and see the points you could earn
  • Works offline, with the last points and program it saw
  • Only looks: it can't give, take or spend points

For staff

Pont Terminal

Turn a staff phone into a card terminal. It needs a pairing code from an admin, once.

  • Give points: type an amount or tap a preset, then hold the card to the phone
  • Check people in and out of an area, with the time they stayed and the points it paid
  • Info kiosk: a tap shows the card's points, nothing is changed
  • Cancel the last charge, check-in or check-out with one button
  • A card that isn't registered can be suggested to an admin with one tap
  • Shows the card's photo and name at every tap, and keeps a list of recent activity

Android 8 or newer, with NFC.

For admins

Pont Admin

The admin site as an app, laid out for the phone. You sign in with an admin account.

  • Everything the admin site has: cards, terminals, readers, check-in areas, transactions, staff and the event program
  • Scan a card to find it, or to fill in its number when adding a card or giving someone a new one
  • Sign in by holding a key (a card or tag) to the phone, and stay signed in for a week
  • Excel exports are saved in your Downloads folder

Android 8 or newer, with NFC.
