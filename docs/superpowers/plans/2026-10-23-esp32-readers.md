# ESP32 readers Implementation Plan

> Executed inline (superpowers:executing-plans): TDD, ledger, one fresh whole-feature review at the end.

**Goal:** ESP32 + MFRC522 + TM1637 readers that register themselves, are configured in admin as check-in points or fixed-amount givers, and show the balance with a line-fill animation.
**Spec:** "ESP32 readers" in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-pont-atm-design.md`.

## Review Focus
- A device must not be able to impersonate another (wrong secret), flood admin with pending rows, or get a token before approval.
- Fixed-amount limits: cooldown, 24 h taps, 24 h points, lifetime taps, per card per reader; stornoed grants stop counting; a replayed tap key grants once; concurrent taps.
- Phones must not be put in fixed mode and readers not in ATM mode; revoking a reader cuts it off at once.
- Display logic (digits, words, scroll, fill frames) and firmware behavior on network loss, bad JSON, double reads.
